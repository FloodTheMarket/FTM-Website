# FTM Master Context Document

## Company Overview

FTM, short for Flood The Market, is a modern hospitality growth and operations company based in London.

The business helps restaurants, cafes, dessert shops and hospitality brands improve:

- visibility
- customer retention
- online presence
- operational organisation
- customer engagement

through content, systems and growth infrastructure.

FTM is not positioned as a generic social media marketing agency.

The company should feel:

- modern
- operator-led
- business-focused
- premium
- scalable
- system-driven

The long-term vision is to evolve from content agency, to hospitality growth infrastructure company, to advisory/accounting/business systems company, and eventually to potential hospitality SaaS/internal tooling company.

## Target Clients

Primary niche:

- restaurants
- cafes
- dessert shops
- aesthetic hospitality brands
- owner-operated hospitality businesses

Ideal clients:

- already have a strong product
- weak online execution
- poor social media consistency
- poor customer retention systems
- weak review systems
- underutilised growth potential
- modern/ambitious ownership

Avoid:

- struggling businesses with no budget
- old-fashioned operators resistant to change
- businesses with poor products

- modern
- sharp
- strategic
